01 — About

Software for watching the sea, and for a small hospital.

I'm a full-stack developer at WESEE in New Delhi. Since 2024 I've worked on maritime and defence software — first through Neemus Software Solutions, now directly.

Day to day that means map-based tracking interfaces, real-time ingestion pipelines for ship and aircraft feeds, and the networking, deployments and CI/CD that keep them running — including the path that carries live data into an air-gapped environment.

My degree is in Computer Science (AI & ML), and object detection with YOLOv8 is where I started: an internship at DYSL-AT and a heavy-vehicle detection project. Outside work I built and maintain a free hospital platform for a local Ayurvedic trust.
